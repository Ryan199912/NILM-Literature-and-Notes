## **Event Detection**

 a transition between distinct operational states within an electrical signal constitutes an event

电信号中不同运行状态之间的转换构成一个事件。

![img](https://dl.acm.org/cms/10.1145/3814607/asset/0aa96d95-3dfb-4694-9583-a9ba2754dce3/assets/images/medium/csur-2025-0707-f04.jpg)

(1)**Type I (Finite State Machines)**: Binary ON/OFF operation.

(2)  **Type II (Discrete Multi-State):** Multiple discrete states with defined transitions.

(3)**Type III (Continuously Varying State)**: Smoothly modulated power consumption.

(4)**Type IV (Permanent State)**: Persistent operation at constant power (e.g., hard-wired detectors, communication devices) 

方法：

(1)**Expert Heuristics**: Rule-based systems requiring appliance-specific thresholds (e.g., power demand, variation). Primarily effective for high-wattage appliances, these methods dominated early research (1990s–2000s).

(2)**Probabilistic Models**: Statistical frameworks inferring events via learned parameters, necessitating supervised training phases.

(3)**Matched Filters**: Template-matching techniques correlating observed waveforms with known appliance signatures. While training-free, this approach imposes high sampling-rate constraints.
